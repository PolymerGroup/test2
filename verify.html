What the record contains

Purity by RP-HPLC

Area-percent purity at 214 nm, measured on the released lot.

Identity by ESI-LC-MS

Observed mass compared with the theoretical mass, with the delta recorded.

Net content

Gravimetric fill weight against the nominal fill.

Testing laboratory & date

The independent laboratory and the date the lot was tested.
