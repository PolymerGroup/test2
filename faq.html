Frequently asked questions

Ordering, documentation and compliance. Still stuck? Contact us →

Who can order from Peptide Scientific?

We supply qualified research institutions, contract research organisations and analytical laboratories. We do not sell to consumers.

Is this material for human use?

No. All products are supplied for in-vitro laboratory research use only. They are not for human or veterinary use and not for diagnostic or therapeutic use.

What documentation ships with an order?

A batch-specific Certificate of Analysis (HPLC + LC-MS), Safety Data Sheet, Certificate of Origin and, where applicable, a cold-chain packing record.

How do I get a COA for an existing lot?

Enter the lot code on the batch verification page, or email the lot code to abc@abc.com.

Can you synthesise a custom sequence?

Yes. Send your sequence, quantity and analytical requirements through the contact form and we will return a feasibility assessment and quotation.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, with the relevant export documentation. Lead time and shipping terms are confirmed in your quotation.
